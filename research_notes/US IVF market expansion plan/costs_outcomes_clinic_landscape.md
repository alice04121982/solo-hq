## 1. Market size: ART cycles, births, share of births, growth, market value

### Takeaway
The latest CDC-validated year is 2022: 435,426 ART cycles at 457 reporting clinics, 98,289 infants, about 2.6% of US births. SART's newer 2024 data counts 449,772 cycles at 364 SART clinics and, for the first time, more than 100,000 IVF babies in a year. Analyst estimates put US IVF services revenue at about $5.9B (2025) and all US fertility services at about $9.4B (2023), growing roughly 5-7% a year.

### Cited Findings
- CDC 2022: 435,426 ART cycles for 251,542 patients at 457 reporting US clinics. These produced 94,039 live-birth deliveries and 98,289 live-born infants, about 2.6% of all US infants. Of those cycles, 184,423 were egg or embryo banking cycles (all eggs or embryos frozen). 2022 is still the latest full CDC surveillance year shown on the page. — [CDC ART Surveillance](https://www.cdc.gov/art/php/surveillance/index.html); [USAFacts](https://usafacts.org/articles/how-many-ivf-babies-are-born-in-the-us/)
- SART 2024 data (released 2026): 449,772 IVF cycles reported by SART-member clinics, 97,549 cycles with a live birth and 100,158 babies, the first year above 100,000. 364 clinics reported to SART for 2024. More than 97% of births were singletons, twins about 2.6%, triplets 0.04%. About 79% of transfers used elective single embryo transfer. — [ASRM press release](https://www.asrm.org/news-and-events/asrm-news/press-releasesbulletins/for-the-first-time-more-than-100000-babies-born-through-ivf-in-the-u.s.-in-a-single-year/); [ReproductiveFacts](https://www.reproductivefacts.org/news-and-publications/fertility-in-the-news/for-the-first-time-more-than-100000-babies-born-through-ivf-in-the-u.s.-in-a-single-year/); [ASRM SART spotlight](https://www.asrm.org/membership/meet-our-members/member-spotlight/group-spotlight-society-for-assisted-reproductive-technology/)
- SART says more than 95% of US IVF cycles are reported to SART. — [ASRM SART spotlight](https://www.asrm.org/membership/meet-our-members/member-spotlight/group-spotlight-society-for-assisted-reproductive-technology/)
- The "1 in 36 babies" framing (about 2.8%) is used by clinic marketing for the latest SART data. — [Illume Fertility](https://www.illumefertility.com/fertility-blog/ivf-statistics)
- SART also publishes a year-by-year trend of cycles, deliveries and babies. — [SART: Number of Cycles, Deliveries, and Babies](https://www.sart.org/ivf-info/ivf-trends/number-of-cycles-deliveries-and-babies/)
- Market value, US fertility services (Grand View): $9.37B in 2023, projected $14.77B by 2030, a 6.7% CAGR. — [Grand View Research, US fertility services](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/horizon/outlook/fertility-services-market/united-states)
- Market value, US IVF (Grand View): $5.88B in 2025, projected $8.38B by 2033. — [Grand View Research, US IVF outlook](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/horizon/outlook/in-vitro-fertilization-market/united-states)
- Market value, other analysts: Precedence Research projects the US fertility market above $21.36B by 2035. — [Precedence Research](https://www.precedenceresearch.com/us-fertility-market)

### Inferences
- The two cycle totals measure different things. CDC's 435k (2022) covers all reporting clinics. SART's 450k (2024) covers SART members only (about 95%+ of cycles). Together they suggest US cycle volume was roughly flat-to-rising at low single digits from 2022 to 2024, while births passed 100k. The site should label counts as "CDC (2022)" or "SART (2024)" and not mix them.
- Banking cycles are about 42% of CDC cycles (184k of 435k). The US cost calculator therefore needs separate flows for "freeze all / egg freezing" and "fresh transfer."
- Analyst market sizes vary widely by definition. Treat them as indicative only.

### Gaps
- Whether CDC has published 2023 surveillance by Sept 2026 could not be confirmed (cdc.gov was blocked). The search only surfaced a 2023 singleton figure (96.74%) without a confirmed CDC citation.
- No SART 2024 breakdown of banking or egg-freezing cycles was retrieved.

## 2. Typical out-of-pocket costs, regional variation, price transparency, packages

### Takeaway
A self-pay US IVF cycle typically costs $12k-$17k for base services plus $3k-$7k for medication. That is about $15k-$30k all-in, and around $50k for the 2-3 cycles most patients need. Add-ons (ICSI, PGT-A, FET, storage) stack on top. Prices are more transparent than they were, but inconsistently so: a minority of clinics publish full price lists, and multi-cycle refund programs (e.g. Shady Grove Shared Risk) usually quote prices only through financial counselors.

### Cited Findings
- Average IVF cycle about $23,474 (2026). Typical range $15k-$30k+ including medication and common add-ons. Base medical services about $12k-$17k, with a published-price median of about $12,450. — [GoodRx](https://www.goodrx.com/conditions/fertility/ivf-costs); [CareRoute (2026)](https://www.careroute.ai/blog/ivf-cost)
- Medications: $1,500 to more than $7,000; many standard protocols about $2,700; commonly $3k-$7k. — [Carrot 2026](https://www.get-carrot.com/blog/ivf-cost-understanding-the-expenses-of-in-vitro-fertilization); [CareRoute](https://www.careroute.ai/blog/ivf-cost)
- FertilityIQ: the average patient undergoes 2.3-2.7 IVF cycles, spending close to $50,000. — [FertilityIQ Costs of IVF](https://www.fertilityiq.com/fertilityiq/ivf-in-vitro-fertilization/costs-of-ivf) (via search extract)
- FertilityIQ publishes a "Cost of IVF by City" comparison. City-level numbers were not retrievable. — [FertilityIQ by city](https://www.fertilityiq.com/fertilityiq/articles/the-cost-of-ivf-by-city)
- Truly all-in own-egg cycle about $20k-$25k; high-end clinics up to about $36k. — [Gaia cost guide 2026](https://gaiafamily.com/en-us/learn/editorials/the-ultimate-guide-to-fertility-treatment-costs-2026-what-youll-pay-why-it-varies-and-how-to-spend-less)
- Stacked cycle (ICSI, PGT-A, freezing, storage, later FET) reaches $30k-$35k+. — [CareRoute](https://www.careroute.ai/blog/ivf-cost)
- PGT-A about $3k-$6k per batch; FET $4k-$6.5k+ (medication and monitoring often extra); egg freezing $14k-$20k per cycle including medication, plus $500-$1,000/yr storage; donor eggs add about $20k-$35k. — [American Surrogacy PGT](https://www.americansurrogacy.com/parents/embryo-genetic-testing-costs); [CNY egg freezing](https://www.cnyfertility.com/egg-freezing-cost/); [Life IVF donor egg](https://lifeivfcenter.com/blog/cost-of-egg-donation/); [Ovu donor costs 2026](https://ovu.com/fertility-insights/donor-costs-2026-what-you-will-pay-for-egg-sperm-embryo-donation); [CareRoute](https://www.careroute.ai/blog/ivf-cost)
- Regional example: Virginia averages about $24,000 per cycle, ranging from $5,769 (CNY) to more than $30,000. — [CNY Fertility, Virginia](https://www.cnyfertility.com/ivf-cost-virginia/)
- Low-price outliers: West Coast Fertility Center (Orange County) and LARC (Los Angeles) about $5k-$7k per cycle; Arizona's New Direction Fertility about $6,500 and Southwest Fertility Center about $6,700. — [Gaia: most affordable US IVF clinics](https://gaiafamily.com/en-us/learn/editorials/which-ivf-clinics-in-the-u-s-are-most-affordable)
- Price transparency: Kindbody publishes package prices on its pricing page, e.g. IVF about $13,800 in New York; egg freezing packages about $4,500-$7,500; assessments $250-$500. Bloodwork and medications ($3k-$6k) are excluded. — [Kindbody pricing](https://kindbody.com/services-pricing/); [VirtualCareFinder](https://virtualcarefinder.com/providers/kindbody); [Blooming Eve](https://www.bloomingeve.com/clinics/kindbody_new_york)
- Other clinics with public price pages include CNY Fertility (low-cost model with published prices), Washington Fertility Center, Virginia Fertility & IVF packages, and Illume Fertility. — [CNY IVF cost](https://www.cnyfertility.com/ivf-cost/); [Washington Fertility](https://www.washingtonfertility.com/fertility-treatment-costs/ivf-treatment); [VA Fertility packages](https://www.vafertility.com/cost-and-financing/fertility-packages); [Illume cost guide](https://www.illumefertility.com/fertility-blog/ultimate-guide-to-fertility-treatment-costs)
- Shady Grove Fertility Shared Risk 100% Refund: covers up to 6 IVF or donor-egg cycles plus subsequent FETs for a flat fee, with 100% refund if unsuccessful or if the patient withdraws. Price depends on age and services and is quoted by a financial counselor, not published. More than 82% of own-egg participants take home a baby. Available for self-pay patients only. — [Shady Grove Shared Risk](https://www.shadygrovefertility.com/refund-programs-for-infertility-treatment/); [Shared Risk FAQ PDF 2022](https://www.shadygrovefertility.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Shared-Risk-100_-Refund-Program-FAQs-July-2022.pdf)
- Mandates: only about 25 states plus DC require any fertility benefit. — [Ovu fertility tourism](https://ovu.com/fertility-insights/fertility-tourism-2025-ivf-abroad-costs-laws-destinations) (secondary source; verify against RESOLVE/KFF)

### Inferences
- A cost calculator can use a defensible default: base cycle about $12.5k-$15k + medication about $3k-$5k + ICSI about $1.5k-$2.5k (not directly sourced here) + PGT-A $3k-$6k + FET $4k-$6.5k. Present these as ranges and let users override with clinic prices.
- Unlike UK HFEA-listed clinic price lists, many US clinics hide prices behind consults or insurance verification. Collecting prices will mean scraping the minority that publish, plus user- or clinic-submitted quotes.
- Refund or shared-risk programs are a distinctive US product. The finder should flag whether a clinic offers one, and its age limits.

### Gaps
- No sourced standalone ICSI price (commonly cited at about $1,000-$3,000, but not verified here).
- Donor sperm (typically about $1k-$2k per vial) and gestational carrier all-in costs (often $100k-$200k+) were not captured from a citable source in this pass.
- No citable NYC/CA vs Midwest/South comparison was retrieved, because the FertilityIQ city page was blocked.
- No published prices were found for Oma Fertility, Aspira, Pinnacle or multi-cycle packages from US Fertility or IVI-RMA.

## 3. Success-rate data: SART vs CDC NASS vs HFEA; licensing and lag

### Takeaway
US success data comes from two overlapping systems. SART (the ASRM affiliate) publishes clinic summary reports on sartcorsonline.com; member clinics report more than 95% of cycles, with preliminary data about 2 years after the treatment year. The CDC's NASS is the federally mandated system under the 1992 Fertility Clinic Success Rate and Certification Act; its validated clinic reports lag about 3+ years, and it offers downloadable clinic-level datasets. SART reports sit behind website terms of use and a "do not compare clinics" disclaimer. CDC clinic datasets are public downloads, and as US federal works they are generally reusable, which makes CDC the safer licensing basis for a comparison site.

### Cited Findings
- SART Clinic Summary Report (CSR): shows national and clinic-specific IVF success rates by category. The 2024 national summary is available on sartcorsonline.com. — [SART 2024 National Summary](https://sartcorsonline.com/Csr/PublicSnapshotReport?reportingYear=2024); [SART Find a Clinic](https://www.sartcorsonline.com/members/Search)
- Users must agree to the SART Website Terms and Conditions before opening a clinic report. SART says the reports should not be used for direct clinic comparisons because patient selection and treatment approaches vary. — [RMA of New York on SART](https://www.rmany.com/blog/sart-and-ivf-success-rates); [SART: Understanding the Clinic Report](https://www.sart.org/patients/fyi-videos/understanding-the-sart-clinic-report/)
- SART regulates how member clinics advertise their success rates. — [RMA Network](https://rmanetwork.com/blog/patients-need-know-sart/)
- SART also offers the IVF Predictor tool and embryo outcome tables. — [SART Predictor](https://sartcorsonline.com/Predictor/ClinicLanding); [SART Outcome Tables](https://www.sartcorsonline.com/EmbryoOutcome/PublicSARTOutcomeTables)
- CDC NASS: clinic-level datasets can be downloaded for each reporting year since 1995. From reporting year 2020 there are four datasets per year, linkable by ClinicID. They include "Final ART Patient and Cycle Characteristics" and "Final ART Success Rates" (success by clinic, stratified by patient and cycle type). Record-level NASS data is protected under a Public Health Service Act s.308(d) Assurance of Confidentiality and is available only via restricted access. — [CDC Accessing NASS Data](https://www.cdc.gov/art/php/data-access/index.html); [NASS Technical Notes](https://www.cdc.gov/art/php/nass/technical-notes.html); [CDC ART Success Rates](https://www.cdc.gov/art/success-rates/index.html)
- Publication lag example: the 2021 Fertility Clinic and National Summary Report was published in 2023. — [CDC Stacks 2021 report PDF](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/154438/cdc_154438_DS1.pdf)
- SART 2024 data was released in 2026 (see Section 1). CDC's 2022 data is the latest on its surveillance page. — [CDC ART Surveillance](https://www.cdc.gov/art/php/surveillance/index.html)
- A mirror of pre-2025 CDC pages (restoredcdc.org) exists, reflecting concern over federal site changes in 2025. — [Restored CDC](https://restoredcdc.org/www.cdc.gov/art/php/surveillance/index.html)

### Inferences
- **Metrics:** SART's current clinic reports centre on outcomes per intended egg retrieval, including cumulative live births from all transfers from that retrieval. They are split into age bands <35, 35-37, 38-40, 41-42 and >42, with separate tables for donor eggs and FET. This matches HFEA-style age-band display but uses different band cut-offs. (Band structure is from background knowledge; confirm on the SART report page.)
- **Licensing:** CDC clinic datasets are US federal government works, generally public domain, which makes them the lower-risk basis for per-clinic numbers. They are 1-2 years older than SART's. Re-publishing SART figures at scale would need permission or a licence from SART; its terms and site-scraping restrictions could not be read here.
- **Freshness:** SART preliminary data is about 2 years old; CDC final data is about 3-4 years old. HFEA's data is similarly lagged but comes from one statutory regulator. In the US the "regulator" (CDC) publishes more slowly than the professional society (SART), and SART membership is voluntary. About 90 of the roughly 450 clinics reporting to CDC were not in SART's 2024 count (457 vs 364).
- **Eligibility:** there is no US equivalent of HFEA/NHS eligibility criteria. The US equivalent is insurance mandate and coverage status by state and employer.

### Gaps
- Exact SART Terms of Use text on reuse was unavailable (sart.org blocked). Confirm directly with SART whether third-party display is licensed.
- CDC release status for 2023 data as of Sept 2026 is not confirmed.
- CDC dataset download URLs and formats (data.cdc.gov) are not confirmed.

## 4. Clinic landscape: counts, SART membership, networks, PE consolidation, access deserts

### Takeaway
About 450-460 clinics report to CDC, and about 364 report to SART. Private equity now shapes much of the market: PE-affiliated clinics grew from 4% (2013) to 32% (2023) of clinics and performed 52% of 2022 cycles. The main platforms are US Fertility, IVI-RMA (KKR, about $3B, 2023), Inception/Prelude, Pinnacle, CCRM, with Kindbody as a hybrid. Clinic acquisitions slowed in 2025, while exits are being explored. About 42% of US women aged 15-50 live more than 30 minutes from a clinic.

### Cited Findings
- Clinic counts: 457 clinics reported to CDC in 2022; 364 reported to SART in 2024. — [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/art/php/surveillance/index.html); [ASRM](https://www.asrm.org/news-and-events/asrm-news/press-releasesbulletins/for-the-first-time-more-than-100000-babies-born-through-ivf-in-the-u.s.-in-a-single-year/)
- PE share: PE-affiliated clinics rose from 4% (2013) to 32% (2023); 52% of all 2022 US IVF cycles were at PE-affiliated clinics. — [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/private-equity-buyouts-fertility-consolidation); [RBEJ 2025 (PE & reproductive medicine)](https://rbej.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12958-025-01446-4)
- Platform layer: US Fertility, IVI RMA, Inception/Prelude, Pinnacle and CCRM dominate, with HRC and Spring as regional specialists and Kindbody as a hybrid. — [CT Acquisitions PE roll-up tracker 2026](https://ctacquisitions.com/guides/fertility-ivf-pe-rollup-tracker-2026/) (M&A-broker source)
- KKR acquired IVI-RMA for about $3B in January 2023. RHWC, Rise Fertility and Halo Fertility began integrating into IVI RMA North America in January 2025. — [CT Acquisitions](https://ctacquisitions.com/guides/fertility-ivf-pe-rollup-tracker-2026/); [Business Wire Jan 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250106667250/en)
- 2025 slowdown: only three US clinics were bought by networks in 2025 (at time of writing). Inception's exit process reportedly reopened, and Kindbody's exit window is open. — [Fertility Bridge 2025 Year in Review](https://www.fertilitybridge.com/news-articles/fertility-industry-year-in-review-2025)
- Access: 41.9% of US women aged 15-50 are more than 30 minutes from an ART clinic. That share is 33.9% in metropolitan tracts, 98.7% in micropolitan tracts and 98.9% in rural tracts. The study covered 451 clinics using 2021 data (J Assist Reprod Genet, 2025). — [Springer JARG 2025](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10815-025-03680-6); [PubMed 40999291](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40999291/)
- 18 million reproductive-aged women lack any regional ART access, and another 7 million have access to only one IVF clinic. States with an IVF mandate have about 6 more satellite clinics per million women and a higher share of women within 60 minutes of a clinic. — [BMC Health Serv Res 2022 (satellite clinics)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12913-022-08281-y); [Fertil Steril geographic access](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0015028217302133)

### Inferences
- A US finder needs a "network/owner" field, since about half of cycles run through PE platforms that often keep local brands (e.g. Shady Grove under US Fertility). It also needs a satellite-vs-lab distinction, since monitoring can happen at satellites far from the lab. The site should support "distance to nearest clinic" search, given the rural deserts.
- Boston IVF, Shady Grove (US Fertility) and Ovation labs (US Fertility) were named in the brief. Their ownership was not independently re-verified here.

### Gaps
- No current authoritative list of states with zero or one clinic was retrieved. Historically these include sparsely populated states such as Wyoming and Alaska; verify with the CDC clinic list.
- Clinic counts per network (e.g. US Fertility, Inception) as of 2026 are not confirmed.

## 5. Low-cost / new models, fertility tourism, medication pricing

### Takeaway
Cheaper models range from $5k-$7k published-price clinics (CNY, New Direction, LARC) to transparent-pricing chains (Kindbody). Cross-border IVF in Mexico costs about $4k-$8k per cycle. The October 2025 White House/EMD Serono deal lists Gonal-f, Ovidrel and Cetrotide on TrumpRx (live January 2026) at up to 84% off, saving up to about $2,200 per cycle for eligible self-pay patients.

### Cited Findings
- TrumpRx / EMD Serono (announced 16 Oct 2025): Gonal-f, Ovidrel and Cetrotide, which typically cost about $5,000 per cycle together, discounted up to 84% when all three are used in a standard protocol. The White House cites savings of up to $2,200 per cycle. The TrumpRx.gov direct-purchase platform launches January 2026. — [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2025/10/16/politics/trump-ivf-emd-serono-trumprx-drug-price); [Fierce Pharma](https://www.fiercepharma.com/pharma/merck-kgaas-emd-serono-strikes-mfn-deal-trumps-fertility-treatment-pricing-push); [Healio](https://www.healio.com/news/womens-health-ob-gyn/20251017/white-house-announces-deal-to-lower-ivf-drug-prices); [EMD Serono press release](https://www.emdserono.com/us-en/company/news/press-releases/agreement-with-u-s-government-to-expand-access-to-ivf-therapies-16-10-2025.html)
- Reported eligibility: income below about 550% of the federal poverty level (about $86k for a single person). This comes from secondary sources only and needs verification. Self-pay medication cost is reported at about $1,500-$3,500 per cycle, down from $4k-$6k. — [TrumpRx IVF explainers](https://www.ivfpharmacy.com/blog/trumprx-ivf-medications-2026/); [Rejuvenating Fertility](https://www.rejuvenatingfertility.com/post/trumprx-ivf-will-it-actually-lower-your-medication-costs-in-2026); [Fertility Bridge](https://www.fertilitybridge.com/news-articles//trumprx-ivf-drug-discounts)
- Low-cost clinics: see Section 2 (West Coast Fertility, LARC, New Direction, Southwest Fertility, CNY about $5.8k). — [Gaia](https://gaiafamily.com/en-us/learn/editorials/which-ivf-clinics-in-the-u-s-are-most-affordable); [CNY](https://www.cnyfertility.com/ivf-cost-virginia/)
- Gaia is a financing/plan company, not a clinic. It offers plan-based financing for IVF and egg freezing. — [Gaia](https://gaiafamily.com/en-us/learn/editorials/which-ivf-clinics-in-the-u-s-are-most-affordable)
- Mexico: own-egg IVF costs about $4,000-$8,200 per cycle vs $15k-$25k in the US; some clinics start at $3k-$4k before medication. Main hubs are Tijuana, Cancún, Mexico City, Guadalajara and Mexicali. Typical stay is 8-12 days, and border patients cross back and forth for monitoring. Mexico has about 17-18% of Latin American fertility cycles. — [Ovu IVF in Mexico 2026](https://ovu.com/fertility-insights/ivf-in-mexico-2026-medical-tourism-costs-fertility-clinic-guide); [Tucson Sentinel/AP](https://www.tucsonsentinel.com/local/report/010224_mexico_ivf/americans-cross-us-mexico-border-more-affordable-ivf-treatment/); [CNY Mexico comparison](https://www.cnyfertility.com/ivf-cost-in-mexico/)

### Inferences
- The TrumpRx deal cuts medication, typically the second-largest cost line, for eligible self-pay patients. A calculator should offer a "TrumpRx eligible" medication toggle, but confirm the eligibility rules first.
- Mini-IVF and INVOcell are relevant low-cost options, but no citable 2026 pricing was retrieved.

### Gaps
- Oma Fertility, Aspira and Pinnacle pricing or business model: no reliable source found.
- Mini-IVF and INVOcell prices: not captured.
- Whether other drug makers (e.g. Organon, Ferring) joined TrumpRx: not verified.

## 6. Financing: loans and grants

### Takeaway
Specialty lenders include Future Family, CapexMD, Sunfish, Prosper Healthcare Lending, LendingClub Patient Solutions and Gaia. Rates range from promotional 0% plans to about 36% APR. Charity grants are small and competitive: BabyQuest $2k-$16k, Cade Foundation up to $10k.

### Cited Findings
- Lenders named across clinic financing pages: LendingClub Patient Solutions, CapexMD, Future Family, Sunfish, Prosper Healthcare. — [Aspire Fertility financing](https://www.aspirefertility.com/fertility-cost/fertility-financing); [Pacific Fertility Center](https://www.pacificfertilitycenter.com/financing-fees/financing-program)
- Prosper: APR 8.99%-35.99%, terms 2-6 years, origination fees 1%-9.99%. The average 3-year APR for loans funded in Q1 2026 was 24.19%. Soft pull with no hard pull for offers under $35k; funds within 1 business day. — [Prosper fertility loans](https://www.prosper.com/personal-loans/healthcare-financing/fertility-ivf)
- Future Family offers 0% APR options and partners with Kindbody. — [Global Loan Advisor 2026](https://globalloanadvisor.com/personal-loans/uses/ivf-fertility/); [Kindbody/Future Family](https://kindbody.com/future-family-partners-with-kindbody-to-expand-access-to-fertility-care/)
- Sunfish finances IVF, egg freezing, surrogacy and donor eggs. — [Sunfish](https://www.joinsunfish.com/blogs-events/apply-for-these-top-5-ivf-fertility-grants-and-financial-aid-programs)
- BabyQuest Foundation: grants of $2,000-$16,000. The Winter/Spring 2026 cycle opened 2 Jan 2026 and closed 12 Mar 2026. — [BabyQuest](https://babyquestfoundation.org/applying-for-a-grant-2/); [GrantExec](https://grantexec.com/grants/fdba2340-a496-490c-ac93-556cc252b9a0)
- Tinina Q. Cade Foundation Family Building Grant: up to $10,000, twice a year (spring and fall). — [Cade Foundation](https://cadefoundation.org/familygrant)
- RESOLVE maintains a directory of grants and scholarships. — [RESOLVE](https://resolve.org/learn/financial-resources/fertility-treatment-scholarships-and-grants/)

### Inferences
- Lender and grant listings are a natural affiliate and monetisation channel for a US site, unlike the NHS-funded UK context. Any lender referral would bring US consumer-finance and FTC disclosure obligations.

### Gaps
- CapexMD and Sunfish current APR ranges, and Future Family's current terms, are not verified.
